Questions
Do I need to be good already?

No. The first two modules assume nothing. If you're already Master or Legend, the intake tells the plan to skip ahead in pace and the later modules are where your rank is hiding.

PC or mobile?

Both. Every lesson has notes for PC and touch where they differ, and the intake asks what you play on so the plan and the 1-on-1 fit your setup.

How long does it take?

That's up to your pace. Train every day and you'll finish the lessons in about three weeks. Once a week and it's a few months. The plan shows your finish date and the dashboard tracks it.

Are all the videos out?

The reading, key points and homework for every lesson are live now, so the course works from day one. Videos are being recorded lesson by lesson and appear in your dashboard as they land. You pay once and get all of them.

What happens on the 1-on-1?

You book it from the dashboard once every lesson is complete. We meet on Discord, you play or we go through a recorded game together, and you leave with a short personal fix-list. If you're not Mythic yet after that, we keep going.
